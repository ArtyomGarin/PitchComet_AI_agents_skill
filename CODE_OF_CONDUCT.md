# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The summary below is not a replacement for the full text.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

* Be kind and patient; assume good intent.
* Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
* Write for readers of different languages: plain words, short sentences.
* Respect people's time: small, focused issues and pull requests.

## Unacceptable behaviour

* Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks.
* Sexualised language or imagery.
* Publishing other people's private information without permission.
* Spam, vote manipulation, or promotion unrelated to the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers through the repository's private reporting
(**Security → Report a vulnerability** can be used for conduct reports as well) or by a direct
message to a maintainer. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove
comments, commits and issues, and may ban contributors temporarily or permanently, following the
enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
